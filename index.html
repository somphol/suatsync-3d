    About this studio

    Made for a daily chant

    SuatSync 3D pairs the Chinabunchorn Chant with a calm, line-by-line reading surface. It is a practice aid, not a teacher: the Pali follows the traditional Thai recension, and the meanings are plain-language glosses.

    Nothing is uploaded. Preferences, calibration, rounds and streaks live in this browser’s localStorage. The YouTube player is only created after you press Connect, and Three.js is loaded from a CDN.
