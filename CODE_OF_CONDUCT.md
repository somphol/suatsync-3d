# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a calm, useful place for people of any background, Buddhist or not, Thai or
not, lay or ordained. We pledge to make participation harassment-free regardless of age, body size,
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity
and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that builds this place:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respecting differing interpretations of a devotional text, and distinguishing opinion from citation.
- Giving and receiving feedback about code or copy without making it personal.
- Crediting sources and translators.
- Accepting that maintaining a small project takes time.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, insults, sexualised attention, or deliberate intimidation.
- Discriminatory jokes or imagery.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Proselytising, sectarian attacks, or using the chant to sell something.
- Sustained disruption of issues, discussions or reviews.

## Scope

This code applies in the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions and any space where someone
represents the project. It does not govern private practice or unrelated social media.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer through the contact on the GitHub profile. Reports are
reviewed promptly and kept confidential. Consequences may include a warning, removal of content,
temporary restriction or a permanent ban, depending on severity and pattern.

The maintainer will avoid conflicts of interest and will explain decisions where it is safe to do so.
